# Nothing on a bot's avatar: no ribbons, no dots

- Date: 2026-11-01
- Source: Alex, relayed by the coordinating session. On the 发布经理 profile's
  large avatar, the coloured ribbons circling the bot are wrong and must go;
  coloured lines on an avatar are not wanted at all. On the dots on the bot
  list's avatars: 「头像上不加任何点」, they should never have been added.
- Rule: R-36
- Replaces: in the `decisions.md` row of 2026-10-31 on idle avatar animations,
  play, orbit, comet and notify (struck there); the bot list's green/orange
  status dot

A bot's avatar is the bloub body and nothing else, everywhere (profile, bot
list, chat header, search, the Coordinator, the first-run guide). No switch
brings any of this back.

- No ribbons or coloured lines. bloub's `orbit` (the body spins while
  coloured ribbons circle it), `play` (a coloured swoosh) and `comet` (a
  coloured trail) are not used. The idle montage keeps idle, egg, wink,
  hexagon and wide; the first-run guide plays `wide` where it played `comet`
  and nothing where it played `orbit`.
- No dots. bloub's `notify` pose draws a badge at the avatar's upper right, so
  a bot with a thread waiting on you no longer takes that pose; its face
  stays idle. The bot list's status dot at the avatar's lower right (green
  while working, orange while waiting on you) is removed. The waiting thread
  still shows in the bot's thread list, and a working bot still blinks and
  widens its eyes.
- The unread dot at the end of a bot-list row is not on the avatar and stays.
  The 「!」 that `exclaim` (出错) draws is the body's own shape, not a dot on it,
  and stays.
