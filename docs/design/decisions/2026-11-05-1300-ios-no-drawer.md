# iOS: no side drawer; the avatar opens an account sheet

- Date: 2026-11-05
- Source: Alex, on #1386 item 8 (relayed by the coordinator): iOS has no native side drawer, so go fully native,
  as in iMessage and Mail.
- Rule: —
- Replaces: the drawer in [2026-11-01-0045](2026-11-01-0045-mobile-home-bar.md) and
  [2026-10-31-2200](2026-10-31-2200-mobile-native-ios.md); the drawer row of
  [2026-10-31-2010](2026-10-31-2010-ota-updates.md) moves to the sheet.

- The app is the system tab bar (对话 · 线程 · 搜索). Each tab's root is a list; a row pushes a chat or thread onto
  the native stack, with the system back button and swipe.
- The person's avatar sits on the left of every tab root's navigation bar. It opens the **account sheet**, a system
  page sheet as in App Store and Music: the person (→ 个人资料), 工作区 (→ switching), 建议 with its count, 任务,
  插件, 设置, and 「重启以更新」 once an update has downloaded. On iOS the list is the system's grouped Form.
- Swipe down closes the sheet; a row closes it and opens its page over the tabs.
- No swipe-right gesture on a tab root; the custom drawer, its gesture and its spring are gone.
