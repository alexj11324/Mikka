# The star is the pin, for every bot; the Coordinator can be unpinned

- Date: 2026-11-01
- Source: Alex, through the coordinator session: 「星标就是置顶标记，所有 bot
  一致」, and 「Coordinator 是普通 bot」. Also: the 没有可用电脑 card's
  title and what it asks the person to do.
- Rule: —
- Replaces: the star drawn only on the Coordinator, and the Coordinator being
  always pinned (decision 2026-11-01-0050-mobile-chat-settings)

- In 对话 on the phone, every pinned bot sits in the pinned row with the star.
  An unpinned bot has no star and sits in the list, the Coordinator included.
- The Coordinator is pinned until the person unpins it. Its page has the
  置顶 switch like any bot. The pin is kept in `chat_preferences` like any
  other, and changing only 通知 keeps it pinned.
- The card for a task no machine can take is titled **没有可用的电脑** (en:
  No computer available). Under the title is one line saying what to do
  first: 先连接或开启一台电脑，任务才能开始运行。 This holds on the web and on
  the phone. The rows below it (enable this computer, E2B key, server) stay as
  they were.
