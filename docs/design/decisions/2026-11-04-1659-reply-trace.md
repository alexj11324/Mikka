# A reply's thinking and tools are shadcn/ui's Trace, as its docs show it

- Date: 2026-11-04
- Source: Alex, GitHub issue #1235 and the session that followed: first affectUI's Thinking Loader, then 「直接用它的 SHADCN 的 trace … 照着官网的来就行了，不要做任何改动。」 (https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/base/trace)
- Rule: —
- Superseded in part by: [2026-11-04-2105](2026-11-04-2105-reply-trace-in-order.md) (tools and words follow their execution order instead of one Trace before the whole reply).
- Replaces: the 「Coordinator 正在处理…」 shimmer line under the chat, and AI Elements' Reasoning and Tool in `ReplyActivity`

What a reply did before its words is a shadcn/ui `Trace` (`components/ui/trace.tsx`), drawn as the Trace docs'
Reasoning and Tool rows draw it, unchanged: a spinner and 「思考中 / Thinking」 while the model thinks, open as its words
stream in; a check and 「思考了 N 秒」 once it stops, closed. A tool runs with a spinner and its label; done, it has a
check and opens to its output in a `CodeBlock`; failed, it is red and opens to its error in a destructive `CodeBlock`.
A reader's own click wins over the automatic open and close. Before a reply has anything to show, the chat ends in the
same Thinking row, running. There is no separate loader.

The model's reasoning streams as it is thought (`lib/coordinator/stream-response.ts`): each piece is masked like the
reply text before it leaves the server, a saved secret is checked whole before any of it is sent, and a retried model
step drops the reasoning it streamed.

The affectUI Thinking Loader (installed under `components/ui/` on the way here) is not used by the chat.
