## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, a bot comments with a link to this
document. Reply on that pull request with the following sentence:

```text
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The bot records your GitHub username, the agreement version and the date. If
you contribute on behalf of an employer or another entity, say so in the same
comment.
